KAYU Privacy Policy
Effective November 5, 2026

KAYU (卡屿) is maintained by WangQvQ. This policy describes the iPhone app and this support website. For questions, use our support page or GitHub issue tracker. The tracker is public; never include private content or credentials.

Your local collection

Cards, selected photos, drafts, your chosen nickname and profile, collection records, and AI creation history are stored on your device. KAYU does not operate a developer server to receive your collection and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. iOS device backups may include app data according to your system settings.

Optional Apple sign-in

You can use KAYU as a guest. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple handles authentication. KAYU stores the Apple user identifier and any provided display name in this device's Keychain. It does not store your Apple password or authentication tokens. Signing out removes the app's saved local sign-in identity; it does not delete your collection or iCloud backups.

iCloud backups

When you explicitly create an iCloud backup, a full-library file is saved to KAYU's dedicated iCloud Drive container using your system iCloud account. It may include card artwork, saved photo originals, drafts, and collection records. Apple provides the transfer and storage. Backups do not automatically merge collections between devices. You can delete backups individually from the backup list.

Optional AI image services

You supply the image service's HTTPS URL, model, and API key. The URL and model are stored locally; the key is stored in this device's Keychain. Before each request, KAYU displays the receiving service and the prompt/reference data to be sent. The request runs only after you consent. Declining does not affect local card creation.

Your prompt and selected reference image are transmitted directly to the service you configure. That provider may also receive network information such as your IP address and applies its own privacy, retention, and billing terms. KAYU does not control that provider. Review its policies before sending personal content. You can remove the service configuration and key in Settings. Keys are not embedded in shared packs or collection backups.

Photos and motion

The system photo picker gives KAYU only the photos you choose. When you ask to save a poster or video, the app requests permission to add that output to Photos. Motion information is used on the device to render card tilt effects; it is not recorded or uploaded.

Importing and sharing

Local files can be imported offline after download. Importing from an HTTPS URL contacts the server you specify, which may receive normal network request information. The system share sheet sends the files you choose to the destination app; that destination handles them under its own policies. Photo cards omit editable photo originals by default when shared; full-library backups include saved originals and drafts. Check the content before sharing.

Retention and deletion

Your local data remains until you remove it or the app's local files are deleted. In Settings, Data Management provides controls to clear local cards, saved originals, drafts, AI history, unopened packs, received files, and recovery copies, while retaining the bundled examples. Apple sign-in identity and image-service credentials have separate removal controls. iCloud backups, exported files, shared copies, and Photos outputs must be managed separately.

Uninstalling removes local app files, but Keychain items and iCloud files may remain. Export a backup outside the app before uninstalling if you want to keep your collection. Restoration creates a recovery copy of the previous library so you can undo the switch.

This website and support

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or forms. GitHub may process technical request information to host and secure the site under the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you submit an issue, GitHub processes your account and issue content; your post is public. Do not upload sensitive files.

Children and changes

KAYU is a general-audience collection tool and is not designed to solicit personal information from children. Do not submit children's private information through public support. If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date. Contact the maintainer through the support page for questions about this policy.

Talk to us

KAYU is maintained by WangQvQ. For app support, bug reports, or privacy questions, open a GitHub issue. Include your app version, iOS version, iPhone model, and steps to reproduce the problem.

GitHub issues are public. Do not post API keys, Apple credentials, private photos, or complete collection backups. Describe privacy requests without including the private content itself.

Common questions

How do I import a pack?

Download the complete .kayu file to the iPhone Files app. Open it with KAYU, or use local file import in Packs. It appears in Packs unopened. Unwrap it to add the cards to your collection. A single card also travels as a pack. Once downloaded, local import works offline.

Can I use a file downloaded from a cloud drive?

Yes. Save the actual file to Files first, then import it locally. A cloud-drive sharing page is not itself a card pack. Downloading from the drive may need its own app or network access; KAYU does not sign in to your cloud-drive account.

How do I make a card from my own picture?

Open Create and choose your own picture. Adjust the layout, finish, lettering, and message, then save it to your collection. For layered cards, provide aligned subject and background images or use the creator Skill to prepare them.

Where do I set up AI generation?

AI is optional. In the app's image-service settings, enter your provider's HTTPS URL, compatible model name, and API key. Before each request, KAYU shows the receiving service and what will be sent. Only continue if you agree. Provider fees and data handling follow your chosen service's terms. No service key or AI credits are included.

Do I need to sign in?

You can use the app as a guest. Sign in with Apple is optional. iCloud backup uses your device's iCloud account and requires iCloud Drive to be available.

How do I back up or move my collection?

In Settings, use Backup & Restore to export the whole library. Save the backup outside KAYU, or explicitly create an iCloud backup and wait until upload completes. Check your backup before changing phones or uninstalling. Restoration replaces the local library; a recovery copy lets you undo the switch. Backups do not automatically merge collections across devices.

How do I delete my data?

Settings offers separate controls to clear local collection data, remove the Apple sign-in identity saved on this device, and delete the image-service configuration and key. Delete iCloud backups separately in the backup list. Shared files, exported backups, and items saved to Photos must be removed from their own locations. Uninstalling can leave Keychain items and iCloud backups in place.

Can I change the language or card labels?

Choose System, English, Simplified Chinese, or Traditional Chinese in Settings. Card titles and creator-written stories keep their original language. In a card's appearance controls, you can independently show or hide the native name and serial-number overlays. Lettering already baked into an illustration stays part of the artwork.
